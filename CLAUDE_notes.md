# Guidelines

## Purpose
These are knowledge and data projects. The work lives in notes, documents, and structured data rather than code.

## Common Project Examples:
- Research a topic in depth and produce a report, summary, or resource
- Create and maintain a wiki or knowledge base
- Maintain organized notes and data for a specific activity (job search, hobby, etc)

## Medium
- Notes in Markdown or plain text
- CSV or other structured data formats
- SQLite when appropriate

## Accessibility
- All notes and data should be readable by both humans and machines
- Avoid formats that limit accessibility

## Organization
- Keep files organized in folders so humans can navigate easily
- Use a project-level `CLAUDE.md` file to explain the project's purpose, current status, and key information.
- Use a `TODO.md` file to track tasks and progress
- Keep these files up to date, and read them at startup in case they've changed.

## What to Avoid
- Code, unless needed for automation or data processing.
- Complex databases or systems unless absolutely necessary
- Proprietary formats or tools
- Unnecessary complexity — keep things simple and clear

## Goals & Focus
Goals may evolve over time. Review and update the project's purpose and focus areas regularly.


## Skills and Tools
- Various Claude skills and tools may be relevant depending on the project's nature.
- If a particular one is unavailable, but would be useful, request it.
